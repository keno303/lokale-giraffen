Giraffe duel

Two giraffes run side by side, without model names. Rotate them, zoom in, watch the walk and pick the better one. You can change your pick until you submit; only then does the page show which models made them. All votes form a ranking.

To keep the ranking from being manipulated, Cloudflare Turnstile checks at the start that a human is voting. For this the page loads a script from Cloudflare. Stored are the votes and a hash of the IP address that changes daily, no cookies.
