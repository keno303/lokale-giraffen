FIELD NOTES / 001

Built for the treetops.

A giraffe’s long neck has just seven vertebrae—the same number as ours. Its two fur-covered horns are called ossicones.

At a walk, the legs on one side move in close succession, followed by the other side. This lateral-sequence gait gives the animal its gentle, rolling rhythm.
