FIELD STUDY / 001

A study in motion.

Long limbs. A measured rhythm. A remarkably gentle presence. This interactive portrait celebrates the reticulated giraffe and its East African home.

The giraffe is procedurally built in Three.js: an irregular, reticulated coat, articulated limbs with inverse kinematics, a four-beat lateral walking gait, and subtle secondary motion in its neck, ears, and tail.
